About Fruit Frenzy

Welcome to Fruit Frenzy, a browser-based HTML5 fruit-slicing game designed for quick and enjoyable play.

Our Game

Slice fruits, build combos, beat your high score, and avoid bombs in a fast-paced 60-second challenge.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a simple, accessible, and enjoyable gaming experience that people can play directly in a web browser without downloading an app.
